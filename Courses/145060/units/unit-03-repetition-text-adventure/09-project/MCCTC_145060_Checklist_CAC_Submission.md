# Congressional App Challenge: Submission Checklist
## 145060 Programming · Unit 3 · Deadline week, October 19-26, 2026

**Why this exists.** A competition submission fails for boring reasons far more often than for bad code: a
missing link, a video nobody can open, a form field left blank, a repository that only runs on one laptop. This
checklist catches the boring reasons so your work gets judged on its merits.

**What this checklist is not.** It is not the rules. The Congressional App Challenge publishes its own rules,
eligibility requirements, required materials, and deadline, and they can change from year to year. **The official
site is the only source for those.** Read it yourself, with your team, before you check off anything below.

**Official site:** `https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/` **[VERIFY]**. Confirm this address with your
instructor before you rely on it, and never enter your information on a site you reached from a search ad or a
link somebody sent you.

**The course syllabus lists the deadline as October 26 at 12:00 pm ET.** Class ends at 10:22. If you are
submitting, plan to be finished **during class on Monday or earlier**, and confirm the official deadline and time
zone on the official site this week.

---

## Part 1 · Read the official requirements (Monday, October 19)

- [ ] Your whole team has read the current official rules page, not a summary of it
- [ ] You have written down, in your own words, **every required item** the official site lists for a submission
- [ ] You have written down the **official deadline, with its time and time zone**
- [ ] You have checked the official eligibility requirements for every team member, and asked your instructor about
      anything you are unsure of
- [ ] You know which parts of registration require a parent or guardian, if any, according to the official site
- [ ] You have asked your instructor any question the official site does not answer. **Do not guess at a rule.**

## Part 2 · Your app works for a stranger (Tuesday to Thursday)

- [ ] Somebody who has never seen your app cloned your repository on a different machine and ran it using only your README
- [ ] The README says what the app does, who it is for, and how to run it
- [ ] Every item your team wrote down in Part 1 exists, and is in the form the official site asks for
- [ ] If you recorded a video or took screenshots: they show the app running, the audio is clear, and anyone can open them
      with the sharing setting the official site requires
- [ ] Every link you will submit opens in a private browser window where nobody is signed in

## Part 3 · Program rules that apply to every project (Thursday)

These are course rules, not competition rules. They apply no matter what the competition allows.

- [ ] **No personal information in any AI tool,** at any point in building the app
- [ ] **No personal information in the repository**: no real names of other people, addresses, phone numbers, or student data
- [ ] **No API keys, passwords, or tokens** anywhere in the repository or its history
- [ ] Your AI usage log is complete: prompt, response, what you changed, and why
- [ ] **Every team member can explain every part of the app.** The course standard applies to competition work too
- [ ] Anything you did not make yourself, such as images, sounds, or code, is allowed by its license and credited

## Part 4 · Submit (by the end of class Monday, October 26, or earlier)

- [ ] Every required field on the official submission form is complete
- [ ] You re-read the whole submission once, slowly, before pressing submit
- [ ] Your instructor has seen the submission **before** you press submit. Assume a submission cannot be changed afterward unless the
      official site says otherwise
- [ ] You saved the confirmation page or confirmation message the site shows you
- [ ] You told your instructor you submitted, and showed the confirmation

## Part 5 · After you submit

- [ ] Your final commit is pushed, and it matches what you submitted
- [ ] Your team wrote three sentences in your decision log: what you are proud of, what you cut, and what you would build next
- [ ] Your text adventure v1 is still due **Tuesday, October 27**. Put it back on your plan for Monday's Build 2
